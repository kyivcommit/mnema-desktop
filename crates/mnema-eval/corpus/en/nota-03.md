# Harrowfield Trust coordination meeting, map digitisation programme

Six of us met on a video call for just over an hour, to close out the second year of the map digitisation programme and agree what happens when Priya Raghunathan steps away from it. Present were Priya, Marcus Oyelaran, two trustees, our archivist counterpart in Nizhyntsi, and a note-taker. These notes went round the same afternoon and nobody objected to them.

The courier collected forty-one crates in the first shipment, and the manifest was signed on the spot. Everything in that load was flat material under A2, which is why it travelled so cheaply; anything larger will need a different carrier and a quote we do not have yet. Insurance was arranged through the trust's existing policy rather than separately.

A transition window of six weeks was agreed, running from the middle of May. Marcus argued for longer and was overruled on cost rather than on principle, and the minute should record that the shorter window was a budget decision. If it slips, the trustees would much rather hear about it in week three than in week six.

Funding for the coming year is confirmed at fifty-two thousand pounds, flat against this year. The archivist asked whether any of that could be moved to cloud storage for the partner side, and the answer was that the grant conditions tie it to equipment and freight. A separate application would be needed, and nobody in the room volunteered to write it. The trustees noted that freight has been the fastest-growing line two years running, and asked for it to be shown apart from equipment in future reports.

Two risks were raised and neither was closed. The older scanning equipment is failing in ways that look like a power problem at the partner site, and the cataloguing backlog for handwritten cards is much larger than last year's estimate. Both were carried forward to the next meeting with named owners.

Actions were assigned as follows. Marcus is to publish a revised rota, Priya is to finish her handover note, and the note-taker is to circulate a corrected attendance list. The next meeting was set for the second Tuesday of June, and nothing else was raised under any other business.
