# Handover note for the map digitisation programme

I am writing this in my last full week, and it covers the things that are not in the runbook because nobody thought to put them there. Read it once now and again in a month, when the parts that sound like superstition will have started to make sense. Where I say the overnight batch below, the older notes and the rota both say the nightly export; they are one and the same job. Marcus has a copy and has already argued with two paragraphs of it.

The certificate renewal script fails silently whenever the upstream registry answers slowly, and nobody gets an alert. It has bitten us three times, always on a Sunday, and the symptom people notice first is that image uploads from the partner site start rejecting. The cure is to rerun it by hand; the real cure is a timeout and a pager rule, which I never got funded.

Marcus Oyelaran owns the overnight batch from June onward, and his name already sits on the duty rota. He has run it beside me since March, so this is a formality rather than a leap, but he has not yet seen a month-end close on his own. Give him the first one with somebody awake nearby.

Credentials live in the shared vault under the programme's own folder, and the trustees' access there was reviewed in April. The one account that is not in the vault belongs to the freight portal, because the supplier refuses anything but a shared login; it is written down in the safe instead. Rotate it when you can, and tell the trustees you did.

Two of the older scanners drop their USB connection under load, so restarting them mid-batch is routine rather than alarming. Losing a page that way is not the same as losing it, since the queue retries from the last confirmed page, but the log will look frightening for a while. Do not open a supplier ticket until it happens twice in one day.

The second consignment left on the fifteenth with nineteen boxes, all of them oversized material the first carrier would not take. My own view is that nine weeks would have been a saner transition than the one that was agreed, and I said so at the time. Everything unfinished is listed at the end of the runbook, in the order I would do it.
