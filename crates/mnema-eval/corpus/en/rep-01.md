# Lanterna quarterly report, third quarter

Lanterna is the reporting platform Northgate builds for logistics operators, and this report covers July through September. The figures below come from the billing export and the availability board, both frozen on the last day of the quarter. Where a number here disagrees with a dashboard, the export wins.

Revenue from logistics customers grew to four hundred and twelve thousand euros, up nine percent on the previous quarter. Two large accounts moved from monthly to annual billing, which flatters the figure a little; adjusted for that shift the underlying growth is closer to six percent. No account of any size cancelled during the period.

Availability finished the quarter at ninety-nine point four percent, below the target we published to customers. Unplanned downtime across the quarter totalled fifty-one minutes, almost all of it in a single August event that has its own review. A second, much smaller interruption in July lasted under four minutes and was absorbed by retries.

Support load stayed flat at roughly nine hundred tickets, though the mix changed: fewer questions about report layout, more about export limits. Median first response held at three hours, and no ticket waited longer than a working day for a human. The team closed the quarter with nine people.

Spending on infrastructure and vendors came to one hundred and six thousand euros, four thousand under plan. The largest single line is the message queue we rent from Sablewire, whose contract comes up for renewal in November; a separate assessment scores that relationship in detail and is not summarised here.

Northgate added two engineers in September and moved the on-call rotation to a follow-the-sun pattern. Both changes were argued for before the August event rather than because of it, and the hiring had been approved in June. Next quarter the team intends to finish the queue migration, publish a revised availability target, and cut the export backlog that grew all summer.
